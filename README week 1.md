# Week 1: Strategic Planning and Data Exploration in Logistics

## Objective
Define a logistics business problem, identify key performance indicators (KPIs), and plan how data science can help evaluate delivery performance.

## Business Problem
Logistics operations may face late deliveries, high transportation costs, traffic-related delays, and difficulty estimating delivery times.

## Project Objectives
- Identify the delivery-performance problem.
- Define measurable logistics KPIs.
- Identify required data fields.
- Plan data preparation, visualization, and predictive modeling.

## Planned KPIs
- **On-time delivery rate:** percentage of shipments delivered within planned time.
- **Average delivery time:** average actual delivery duration.
- **Average delay:** difference between actual and planned delivery time.
- **Average transportation cost:** average cost per shipment.
- **Cost per kilometre:** transportation cost relative to distance.

## Proposed Methodology
1. Define the business problem and objectives.
2. Collect or generate shipment records.
3. Inspect and clean the data.
4. Explore patterns using statistics and charts.
5. Train and evaluate delivery-time prediction models.
6. Summarize findings and possible recommendations.

## Tools
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Scikit-learn.
